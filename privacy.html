Inside Job privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

Inside Job does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Your progress, settings, achievements and daily job results are saved only on your own device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time from Options, Erase progress, or by deleting the app.

Internet use

The game itself runs entirely on your device and works offline. It does not download updates or talk to any server of ours. New versions arrive through the App Store.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app reports your achievements and two scores (your career reputation and your daily job score) to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never see your Game Center account or details. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

In-app purchase

The Full contract book is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your account details; the app only learns whether the purchase is owned, so it can unlock the extra buildings. Restore purchases asks Apple the same question.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a daily job result, the text goes wherever you choose to send it using your device's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub.
